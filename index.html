How to play

Four simple steps to becoming a pizza maker.

Move

Drag anywhere on the screen to walk around your shop.

Bake

Walk by an oven to pick up fresh pizza boxes.

Serve

Drop pizzas at the counter and collect the cash.

Grow

Spend cash on unlock pads, upgrades and staff.

FAQ

Is Pizza Maker free?

Yes. The game is free to play and is supported by ads.

Can I play without internet?

Yes. You can play offline and no account is needed. Your progress is saved on your device.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones running Android 10 or newer.

Which languages are available?

English, French, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese.

Does my restaurant earn money while I'm away?

Yes! Come back to collect your offline earnings, with a bonus if you want it.
